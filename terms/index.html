About Blobular

Blobular is published by Wiley Evans, operating as Thinknoise. It is an experimental audio instrument for iPhone, with a related Web instrument. You can record or import audio, explore fragment playback, and keep a Local audio library. Optional account-based Cloud features store recordings separately from your device.

Account-based Cloud saving requires service authorization and paid or complimentary access. Subscription availability depends on your App Store region and the service rollout.

Prices in other currencies and any applicable taxes are shown by Apple; review the price and monthly billing period in Apple’s purchase confirmation before purchasing.

App Store subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple may charge for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Cancel through Apple’s subscription controls. You can review or cancel in iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Blobular. You can also open Apple’s subscription settings or follow Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Cancellation stops future renewal; paid access continues through the verified remaining subscription period unless Apple refunds or revokes the purchase. Apple handles payment and refund requests under its applicable terms. Deleting Blobular, signing out, or requesting account deletion does not itself cancel the Apple subscription.

In the iPhone app, signed-in free accounts can experiment with temporary audio, which is not restored after relaunch. Existing Local recordings remain accessible after subscriber access expires. Complimentary access does not require an App Store purchase and has no automatic expiry; an administrator can revoke it.

Existing audio after access expires

After subscriber access expires or is revoked, the account-based Cloud service denies new saves and upload completion. Subscription expiry alone does not delete existing recordings. Authenticated account owners can continue to list, download and delete their existing Cloud recordings, subject to service availability and account access.

Local audio remains separate from subscription status. This describes the current service behavior; it does not promise indefinite Cloud retention or replace keeping your own backups.
