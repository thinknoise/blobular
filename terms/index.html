About Blobular

Blobular is an experimental audio instrument for iPhone, with a related Web instrument. You can record or import audio, explore fragment playback, and keep a Local audio library. Optional account-based Cloud features store recordings separately from your device.

Account-based Cloud saving is currently limited to approved testing. Subscription terms below apply if Cloud subscriptions are made available for purchase through the App Store; this draft does not announce subscription availability.

Local and Cloud storage

Local files live in the iPhone app’s storage. Signing in does not upload or synchronize them automatically. Cloud uploads and downloads create separate copies; deleting a copy in one library does not delete copies elsewhere.

Cloud features depend on account authorization, a working internet connection, supported files and available service capacity. Uploads or downloads can fail, be interrupted, or be rejected because of file or access limits. Cloud access is not a promise of unlimited capacity, permanent availability or a guaranteed backup.

Keep independent copies of important audio and confirm a transfer succeeded before removing an original. For failed transfers, preserve the original and contact support. Data handling and deletion are described in the Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions, billing and cancellation

Where offered, a Blobular Cloud monthly subscription is billed through your Apple Account at the price, currency and billing period shown in Apple’s purchase confirmation. Confirm those details before purchasing. No free trial or additional subscription tier is promised here.

App Store subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple’s subscription controls. You can review or cancel in iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Blobular. You can also open Apple’s subscription settings or follow Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Cancellation stops future renewal; paid access continues through the verified remaining subscription period unless Apple refunds or revokes the purchase. Apple handles payment and refund requests under its applicable terms. Deleting Blobular, signing out, or requesting account deletion does not itself cancel the Apple subscription.

Cloud saving requires enabled access and an active subscription or an administrator-granted complimentary entitlement. Complimentary access does not require an App Store purchase and has no automatic expiry; an administrator can revoke it.

Existing audio after access expires

After subscriber access expires or is revoked, the account-based Cloud service denies new saves and upload completion. Subscription expiry alone does not delete existing recordings. Authenticated account owners can continue to list, download and delete their existing Cloud recordings, subject to service availability and account access.

Local audio remains separate from subscription status. This describes the current service behavior; it does not promise indefinite Cloud retention or replace keeping your own backups.
